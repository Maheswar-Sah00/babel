# Babel: shooting script (2:32)

**Format:** you on camera (A-roll), cut with screen recordings and graphics (B-roll).
**Spoken:** about 380 words, about 2:30 at a calm pace (150 words a minute).
**Demo:** the WEBFISHING demo button on the Start screen (30,000 words, $10,000 budget). It runs from cache, so it
never fails on camera.

Every number you say has a source. The **Evidence** line under each shot says where it comes from; the full list is at
the end, with a table of every place Qloo powers Babel. Say the lines word for word: they were written to match those
sources exactly.

**Shot types:** CU = close-up (face fills the frame) · MCU = medium close-up (head and shoulders) · MS = medium shot
(waist up) · SCR = screen recording · GFX = graphic or animation · VO = your voice over B-roll

**Qloo moments** are marked 🟣. There are five: the idea (3B), the fan map (4D), who the fans are (4E), the "without
Qloo" contrast (scene 5) and the thank-you (7A).

## Where Qloo powers Babel

Use this for the Q-references above, and if a judge asks "what does Qloo do here?".

| # | What Qloo does in Babel | Qloo API used | Where in the code | What you'd lose without it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Finds each look-alike game in Qloo and checks it has real fan data; Qloo's generic "no data" map is detected by its fingerprint and never used | `/search` (`urn:entity:videogame`); `/v2/insights` heatmap fingerprint | `babel/qloo.py` (`find_game`, `fingerprint`, `default_fingerprint`); `babel/comps.py` (`qloo_status`) | No way to know which look-alikes have real fans |
| Q2 | **World fan maps** of the look-alikes, summed per country and turned into a per-language **Qloo adjustment**. This is the only game-specific input to the language scores, and it draws the map | `/v2/insights` with `filter.type=urn:heatmap` over the whole world | `babel/qloo.py` (`world_cells`, `country_scores`, `country_profile`); `babel/plan.py` (lift) | Every game gets the same ranking, and there is no map |
| Q3 | **Who the fans are:** age and gender skew of fans of the look-alikes vs Qloo's average audience | `/v2/insights` with `filter.type=urn:demographics` | `babel/markets.py` (`audience`) | No audience profile |
| Q4 | **What these fans also like, by market:** films and music popular in each top market, re-ranked by the look-alikes' fans | `/v2/insights` with `filter.type=urn:entity:movie` / `urn:entity:artist`, `signal.location.query` | `babel/markets.py` (`also_loved`) | No market notes |
| Q5 | **The proof:** the backtest measured what Qloo adds on top of the typical mix (87 better, 33 worse) | The same heatmaps, frozen in `backtest/data/` | `backtest/evaluate.py`, `backtest/final_report.md`; `killcheck/` | No evidence that the plan is better than the generic list for a given game |

**What "without Qloo" means, honestly** (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, section 1): Babel would still have the typical language
mix, but every game would get the same list, with no map, no audience and no "why". Qloo is what moves the ranking per
game, and that move was measurably better on the larger test. Say it that way; don't say Babel can't plan at all
without Qloo.

## Shooting notes

- **Camera:** eye level, light in front of you (a window or lamp), plain background. A phone is fine; a clip-on mic
  matters more than the camera.
- **Takes:** shoot every camera line three times: neutral, a bit warmer, a bit faster. Pick in the edit.
- **Say "Qloo" clearly:** it's pronounced "clue". Don't swallow it; it's the word the judges listen for.
- **Cuts:** cut on words, not in the middle of them. Keep B-roll shots under 6 seconds.
- **Music:** light and low, ducked almost to silence under your voice.
- **Too long?** Cut 2C first, then 4F. That saves about 8 seconds and keeps every Qloo moment.
